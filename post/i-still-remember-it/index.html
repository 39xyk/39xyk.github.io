I Still Remember it

2026-08-19 1 分钟阅读 # 闲话

I still hold fast to that afternoon,
That glance, that moment, burned into my moon‑lit memory.
The summer days stretched far, beneath the blazing noon.
We should have rested, yet exams called us back to the academy.

Hot wind brushes my cheeks as I ride along the way,
I swipe the card, pass through the gate, all ordinary as before.
Cicadas cry aloud beneath the sun’s unforgiving ray,
Sweat beads upon my forehead, the air is thick and raw.

Soft footsteps drift behind me, through the noisy campus hum,
A name long lingering within my quiet, wandering mind.
I turn my head, and there you come.
Summer light wraps round your frame, gentle and kind.

Breeze tangles your fine hair against your fair skin’s glow,
Pale‑grey frames rest upon your eyes, where soft amusement lies.
Our gazes meet — my racing heart begins to blow,
I look away, and blame the heat for flushing of my eyes.

So many chance encounters have come and gone in years,
Most fade like mist, and leave no trace behind.
But this brief spark of youth, this sight my spirit cheers,
Survives in time, forever fresh inside my mind.
